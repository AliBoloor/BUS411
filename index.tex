\section{Course Overview}\label{course-overview}

Welcome to \textbf{BUS 411: Fixed Income Security Analysis and
Valuation}.

As an introduction to the market valuation of fixed income securities,
this course covers: history of fixed income securities; valuation
methods for fixed income securities, including fixed and variable
annuities, government and corporate bonds, defined benefit pension
plans, bonds with contingencies, fixed income derivative securities;
and, fixed income risk management using duration and convexity measures.

Prerequisite: BUS 315 and BUS 360W, both with a minimum grade of C-; 60
units.
